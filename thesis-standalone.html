Performance Analysis of Real-Time PPP with Ambiguity Resolution in Simulated Scenarios

Open PDF (new tab)

Master Thesis

This thesis investigates the feasibility of applying Ambiguity Resolution (AR) to Precise Point Positioning (PPP) in dynamic simulated scenarios. By implementing and evaluating PPP-AR approach using simulated data, this work significantly reduces conventional PPP convergence time and improves positioning accuracy. Additionally, it details the integration of the AR functionality into the BKG Ntrip Client (BNC) software, providing architectural insights and comparisons with existing open-source methods.
